Growing Good Things in Raglan

At GrowBox Raglan, we believe growing your own food should be simple.

We build vegetable garden boxes and fill them with compost from Raglan’s local waste recycling centre, giving recovered organic material a new purpose and helping more people grow fresh food at home.

Whether you’re starting your first veggie garden or making better use of your backyard, we take care of the groundwork so you can get growing.
